01 • The Story

Every place carries a memory.

“Ti Sao a Nagbalin nga Ili” explores Pagudpud through history and geography, connecting the past with the community and landscape readers can see today.

Mission

This blog aims to inform readers about the history and discovery of the town Pagudpud. Tracing where was the town truly came from. Beyond its renowned white sand beach, beautiful destination or a tourist support. This blog reveals the origin of the town, the story behind it and the significant events that shaped its identity.

“The journey is not only measured by how far our feet can walk, but by how deeply we understand the places we pass.”

For students, the blog turns local history into an interactive experience. For residents, it creates a digital space for remembering community heritage. For travelers, it adds historical meaning to the places they visit.

04 • World War II

When the USS Stingray Came Ashore

A local shoreline can also be part of a global war.

Historical accounts record that the American submarine USS Stingray reached the Caunayan area of Pagudpud on August 27, 1944. A fifteen-man team led by 2nd Lt. Jose V. Valera landed with weapons and equipment intended to support resistance efforts against Japanese forces.

Studying this event helps connect local history with the wider Philippines Campaign of World War II and reminds readers that major historical events are also experienced in particular communities and places.

PIN 01

🪨 Bantay Abot Cave

A distinctive coastal formation. The name is commonly explained as “a mountain with a hole,” and the feature is technically an arched rock formation rather than a conventional cave.

PIN 02

🌊 Dos Hermanos Islands

The “Two Brothers” islets are a recognizable part of the coastal landscape and can be seen from the Maira-ira area.

PIN 03

🌉 Patapat Viaduct

A winding coastal viaduct that follows the northern landscape and forms an important transport connection between the Ilocos Region and Cagayan Valley.

07 • Why Local History Matters

From one town to a bigger story.

Learning about Pagudpud is more than learning about one municipality. Local history helps students understand how communities develop, how people interact with their environment, how historical events reach local places, and why cultural and historical heritage deserves care.
